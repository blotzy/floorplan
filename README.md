**Download the plan as a DXF: [accosting-owner-schematic.dxf](accosting-owner-schematic.dxf)** (units are inches; opens in QCAD, AutoCAD or any DXF viewer). With the survey underneath: [accosting-owner-schematic-survey.dxf](accosting-owner-schematic-survey.dxf).

Walls are drawn at realistic thicknesses: exterior 9" (2x6 + sheathing + 1" rigid insulation + stucco), interior 4½", plumbing walls 6½". Room sizes on the drawing are clear dimensions, finish face to finish face. Sizes and fine details are the architect's call; the drawing shows one way the [wishlist](#design-wishlist-by-trade) fits.

## 3D massing model

**[Open the 3D model](https://blotzy.github.io/floorplan/renders/3d/house.html)** (GitHub Pages; GitHub's file view only shows the source). From a local clone, open `renders/3d/house.html` in a browser instead. It has orbit, zoom, toggle ceiling/furniture, a sun-position control, and a **Walk** button for eye-level navigation (WASD + mouse on a computer; on a phone or tablet, a joystick to move and drag to look). The same geometry is in `renders/3d/house.obj` for Windows 3D Viewer or Blender.

![3D massing preview](renders/3d/preview.png)

![3D exterior: MCM shed with clerestory, stucco](renders/3d/preview-exterior.png)

*The chosen exterior: a midcentury-modern shed roof in dark standing-seam metal, a 3' clerestory band over the lounge, stucco walls, a wood-look feature wall at the porch, and the nearly flat carport roof. Inside, the foyer, lounge, dining and kitchen share one sloped ceiling. The model opens with the roof off; tick **Roof** to show it, and untick **Edge lines** for continuous stucco walls without the outlines between wall pieces, or open [house.html#roof](https://blotzy.github.io/floorplan/renders/3d/house.html#roof). The Roof menu also has the alternatives: the [MCM gable with flat ceilings](https://blotzy.github.io/floorplan/renders/3d/house.html#roof-gable), the metal shed with a [full-height clerestory](https://blotzy.github.io/floorplan/renders/3d/house.html#roof-full), the earlier shingle shed with a [3' band](https://blotzy.github.io/floorplan/renders/3d/house.html#roof-shingle) or [full height](https://blotzy.github.io/floorplan/renders/3d/house.html#roof-shingle-full), and the plain shed with no clerestory and flat ceilings in [metal](https://blotzy.github.io/floorplan/renders/3d/house.html#roof-plain) or [shingles](https://blotzy.github.io/floorplan/renders/3d/house.html#roof-shingle-plain).*
