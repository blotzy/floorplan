Room sizes on the drawing are clear dimensions, finish face to finish face. Sizes and fine details are the architect's call; the drawing shows one way the [wishlist](#design-wishlist-by-trade) fits.

## 3D massing model

**[Open the 3D model](https://blotzy.github.io/floorplan/renders/3d/house.html)** (GitHub Pages; GitHub's file view only shows the source). From a local clone, open `renders/3d/house.html` in a browser instead. It has orbit, zoom, toggle ceiling/furniture, a sun-position control, and a **Walk** button for eye-level navigation (WASD + mouse on a computer; on a phone or tablet, a joystick to move and drag to look). The same geometry is in `renders/3d/house.obj` for Windows 3D Viewer or Blender.

![3D massing preview](renders/3d/preview.png)

![3D exterior: MCM shed with clerestory, stucco](renders/3d/preview-exterior.png)

*The chosen exterior: a midcentury-modern split roof in dark asphalt shingles, a shed from the porch to the gym/ensuite wall with the 3' clerestory band over the lounge, and a low gable over the rest of the wing ([street](renders/3d/exterior-street.png), [east](renders/3d/exterior-east.png) and [rear](renders/3d/exterior-rear.png) views; and eye-level views from the street and driveway: [across the street](renders/3d/street-views/street-across.png), [from the east](renders/3d/street-views/street-east.png), [from the west](renders/3d/street-views/street-west.png), [up the driveway](renders/3d/street-views/driveway.png), [the entry court](renders/3d/street-views/entry-court.png)); stucco walls, a wood-look feature wall at the porch, and the nearly flat metal carport roof. Inside, the foyer, lounge, dining and kitchen share one sloped ceiling. The model opens with the roof off; tick **Roof** to show it, and untick **Edge lines** for continuous stucco walls without the outlines between wall pieces, or open [house.html#roof](https://blotzy.github.io/floorplan/renders/3d/house.html#roof). The Roof menu also has the alternatives: the [MCM gable with flat ceilings](https://blotzy.github.io/floorplan/renders/3d/house.html#roof-gable), a split with the [shed only over the lounge](https://blotzy.github.io/floorplan/renders/3d/house.html#roof-split) with a 15' clerestory, gables either side, a flat 9' foyer ceiling and a beam across the dining room on the foyer-wall line ([street](renders/3d/roof-split-street.png), [east](renders/3d/roof-split-east.png), [rear](renders/3d/roof-split-rear.png), [dining](renders/3d/roof-split-dining.png)), and the [shed over the whole house](https://blotzy.github.io/floorplan/renders/3d/house.html#roof-shed).*
