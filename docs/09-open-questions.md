**Planner / permits**

- [ ] Planner: confirm the side setback is 10% of lot width (11'-3⅝"; the plan uses 11'-6" on the west and 11'-6" to the carport on the east).
- [ ] Planner: does the 100'-frontage street-facing garage rule (§22.140.520) apply to a carport? If so, request a modification under Procedure A.
- [ ] Planner: can uncovered parking in the driveway inside the front setback count as a required space?
- [ ] Driveway: the drawn entrance threads between the parkway street tree and the lot tree; confirm the root-zone clearance with an arborist (and any County requirement for work near a street tree), and drain the drive away from the house at the carport.
- [ ] Architect: final square footage, checked against the rebuild permit's size limit.

**Design decisions (owner)**

- [x] Guest closet: decided. One long closet, with the door at the bath end.
- [x] Primary tub: decided. A back-to-wall freestanding soaking tub (about 5'-7" x 32") in the primary bath wet room; the shared ensuite gets a standard 66" tub with a shower head. Pick both models before framing.
- [ ] Pick the primary back-to-wall tub. The space between the end walls is 6'-0". Base (floor footprint) no longer than about 5'-0" for at least 6" clear at each end at the floor; rim up to about 5'-8" (2"+ at each end) if the sides flare. Also check width (about 30"–32"), drain position, the wall-mounted filler, and the filled weight.
- [ ] Primary tub filler: wall-mounted on the wall behind the tub (shared with the common bath), floor-mounted beside the tub, or on the tub's end wall at the closet side (already a plumbing wall). Only the first keeps the wall behind the tub a 2x6 plumbing wall; otherwise it can be a plain 2x4 wall, and the robot vacuum dock in the storage room takes its water and drain through the floor.
- [x] Kitchen tall storage: decided. Floor-to-ceiling cabinets on both sides of the range.
- [ ] Pick the fridge model (sets the exact opening and door clearance). When shopping, check that its depth and open doors leave the walkway past it clear.

**Trades**

- [ ] Architect and civil engineer: foundation and grading for the slope (about 4 ft of fall across the house footprint, more across the lot): stepped vs. raised slab, how the west-end exterior door and the dining slider meet grade, and drainage around the house.
- [ ] Civil / demolition: remove the old retaining wall under the southeast corner and the standing chimney in the courtyard; deal with the lower rear terrace they leave.
- [ ] East property-line wall (front half of the lot, sits above our grade):
  - It retains the neighbour's soil (their side is higher), so our side is the toe of the wall.
  - [ ] Surveyor: exact position relative to the line (it appears to straddle it).
  - [ ] Condition check after the fire (cracks, lean, bulging, weep holes, heat damage); structural/geotechnical engineer if anything looks off. Repairs to walls over 4 ft (footing to top) or ones holding up a slope or driveway need a permit.
  - [ ] Neighbour: since it holds their soil, maintenance and repair usually fall mainly on them; agree on ownership and responsibilities in writing. If both lots are rebuilding, consider a shared repair or a new noncombustible wall (with a privacy fence on top, if wanted).
  - [ ] Before demolition: photograph it. Architect / civil: no excavation or lowering of grade along its base on our side; a swale or gravel drain along the wall carries its seepage to the site drainage, away from the house and carport.
- [ ] Plumber: confirm the building sewer can reach the existing front cleanout with code slope from the west-end baths, given the lot falls to the west.

- [ ] Architect and insulation contractor: spray foam under the roof deck or rigid board above it, and the matching thermal barrier for storage use.
- [ ] Roofer: written confirmation that the shingle warranty covers an unvented (spray-foam) deck.
- [ ] Truss designer: 500 lb concentrated loads at the gym ring points; 20 psf attic storage strip.
- [ ] Plumber and sprinkler designer: does the sprinkler system require a 1" water service / meter upsize?
- [ ] Plumber: confirm the robot vacuum dock drain connection and trap.
- [ ] HVAC and energy consultant: an ERV instead of the continuous exhaust fan (about $4,000–7,000 more installed): fresh air through a MERV 13 filter on smoke days and some heat and humidity recovery. What does it earn in the Title 24 run?
- [ ] HVAC: Manual J load calculation and Manual D duct design to size the ducted heat pump and confirm three zones (or two, if the primary suite is too small to run alone).
- [ ] Electrician: load calculation with the full appliance list, confirming 200A.
- [ ] Electrician: SCE service point and meter location (west end?) and the EV charger run to the carport.
- [ ] Engineer: shed roof framing over the vaulted great room: the bearing/beam line where the vault meets the flat ceilings (the lounge/gym and kitchen/pantry walls are on one line, so the vault ends on one straight line), the tall street wall with the clerestory band, and lateral bracing along it.
- [ ] Engineer: does the wide dining window (with the dining slider around the southeast corner) need steel framing or a steel hold-down at that corner? If so, reconsider the window size; a narrower window with more wall at each end is the fallback.
