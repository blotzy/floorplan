# Project basics

[← Back to README](../README.md)

All-electric single-story rebuild at 220 Laurel Dr, Altadena (Eaton Fire rebuild) with no gas service, 10' ceilings, a sealed attic, and an attached 18'-wide two-car carport. The architect owns the floor plan, roof, and finishes. Plumbing, HVAC, and electrical are designed by separate trades, so the architect only needs to reserve the spaces, chases, and openings those sections call for.

**Orientation used in these docs:** "north", "east", etc. mean **plan** directions: the street is plan north, the carport and great room are at the plan east end, and the bedroom wing and primary suite at the plan west end. (The plan drawings put plan north at the bottom, so east is on the left.)

**True orientation:** plan north is **21° east of true north**. From the LA County parcel GIS (APN 5832-003-027; parcel 27 on assessor map 5832-003), the street lot line runs at a bearing of 111° and the side lines at ~21°. So the street side faces NNE, the "south" (rear) side faces SSW (azimuth ~201°), the carport end faces ESE, and the primary-suite end faces WNW. The parcel GIS shows the lot as ~112' x 64'-9" (the plan drawing says 113'). The 3D model's sun uses the true orientation.

| Item | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Fuel | All-electric, no gas line to the lot |
| Ceiling height | 10' throughout (no step-down over the bedrooms), with a sealed (unvented) attic above |
| Doors | 8' interior doors throughout |
| Flooring | LVP throughout, flush transitions with no raised thresholds |
| Footprint | House 72' x 31'-8"; carport 18' x 20' on the east end |
| Siting | House pushed west to the side setback: 11'-4" on the west, 11'-8" from the carport to the east line, 20'-1¼" front, 13' rear |
| Bedrooms | Primary + 3 secondary: bedroom 2 = gym, bedroom 3 = guest, bedroom 4 = office |
| Baths | Primary bath, common bath, powder room |
| Code basis | 2022 California codes allowed for fire rebuilds (EO N-29-25); fire sprinklers required (NFPA 13D); Class A roof and Chapter 7A wildfire construction (VHFHSZ) |

The area label on the plan drawing (2,235 sq ft) is out of date. See [open questions](09-open-questions.md).
