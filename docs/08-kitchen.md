# Kitchen: cabinets and appliances

[← Back to README](../README.md)

An upside-down U: the sink on the south wall under the window, the range and fridge on the **west** wall (backing onto the laundry), and a peninsula on the east side with **three stools** facing the dining area. The aisle inside the U is about **4'** (48" is the two-cook minimum).

**West wall, from the sink corner:** 15" counter → 30" induction range → 18–20" counter → 36" fridge (counter-depth or all-refrigerator column) at the north end, next to the pantry door.

| Item | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| Range | 30" slide-in induction |
| Range hood | Ducted outside through the roof, 400 CFM or less |
| Fridge | Space for 36", counter-depth; RO water line and a leak pan |
| Dishwasher | In the peninsula on the kitchen side, at its south end, just around the corner from the sink; not across from the range. |
| Sink | RO filter tap (the tankless RO unit is in the mechanical closet, so the sink base is free for the trash pull-out); integrated drain board on the dishwasher side |
| Peninsula | Three stools; a small under-counter drink fridge at the stool-side corner by the south wall, opening toward the dining room, with the coffee station on the counter above |
| Corner storage | Corner drawers at the range-side corner |
| Pantry | Off the lobby at the corner by the fridge; outlets inside |

**Also add**

- Doors on the dining-room side of the peninsula's corner end, turning the blind corner into a buffet cabinet.
- 24" triple pull-out (trash, recycling, and a small lidded food-scraps bin for LA County's green organics cart) in the peninsula on the kitchen side, right beside the dishwasher: sink → scrape → bin → dishwasher. Make recycling the larger bin.
- Appliance garage with an outlet in the countertop corner.
- Under-cabinet lighting.
- 42" or stacked upper cabinets to suit the 10' ceilings.
- 3" fillers at each corner so drawers and doors clear each other.
- Peninsula seating overhang of 15".

**Tall storage (wanted, not yet placed).** The kitchen is short on counter-to-ceiling cabinets because the sink window takes the south wall and the range/hood takes the west wall. Candidates: a counter-to-ceiling hutch over the prep counter between the range and fridge, a tall pantry column beside the fridge, and/or a built-in on the dining room's south wall.
