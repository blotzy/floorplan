# Electrical

[← Back to README](../README.md)

200A service with a 225A-busbar panel and a 200A main breaker, with conduit from the service point sized for a future 400A upgrade.

**Service and panels**

- Exterior meter-main with a 225A busbar and 200A main (SCE-approved, "solar-ready"), which also serves as the code-required emergency disconnect. Location follows SCE's service point (utilities are at the west end).
- Underground conduit from the service point sized for 400A (roughly 3"), so a future upgrade means new wire and gear, not new trenching.
- Indoor subpanel flush in the laundry's pantry-side wall, just past the laundry door swing, with 30" x 36" of clear working space and 6'-6" of headroom.
- Whole-house surge protector.

**Outlet locations** (standard outlets, no dedicated circuit needed)

- In the mechanical closet, for the tankless RO filter.
- Robot vacuum dock niche.
- GFCI outlet beside each toilet in all three baths, for bidet seats.
- Inside the pantry.
- On the peninsula, under the countertop or on the end panel (code-required).
- Countertop corner, for the appliance garage.
- Behind the TV on the **lounge side of the foyer wall** (the TV wall), in a recessed box.
- Office: outlets at desk height on the desk wall.
- Exterior GFCI outlets outside the dining slider and near the bin pad.

**Boxes and wiring**

- Ceiling-fan-rated boxes in the bedrooms and great room (not in the gym's ring swing zone).
- Vaulted great room (no attic above it): wall outlets and switches are fed from the walls that border the attic and run sideways through the studs, or through slab conduit (the peninsula needs that anyway). Vault lights, fans, smoke alarms and any clerestory shade motors are wired in the rafter bays before the spray foam, with a few spare conduits and pull strings to likely future spots.
- Recessed lights down the centre of the bedroom hall (lobby), between the ceiling trusses, IC-rated and airtight; spaced clear of the attic hatch, smoke alarm and return grille. The framing and HVAC keep that centreline free (see sheet A-7).
- Neutral wires in all switch boxes, for smart switches.
- Door contact sensor and smart lock at the front door.

**Solar and battery prep (installed later)**

- Solar breaker space on the 225A busbar, at the opposite end from the main.
- Solar conduit runs through the interior: from the array through the roof into the attic, then inside the walls to the panel. No conduit on the roof surface or down the outside walls. Leave an empty conduit (with pull string) from the attic to the panel at rough-in, and one from the panel to the future battery location (about 3' clear of doors and windows).
