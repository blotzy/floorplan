# Site, carport, and landscape

[← Back to README](../README.md)

## Siting and carport

- House pushed west to the side setback: **11'-4"** west side yard (10% of the 113' lot width, rounded up; confirm with the planner).
- **Carport 18' x 20'** on the east end, two cars side by side, **11'-8"** from the east property line. Clear-span beam with posts only at the corners (no middle post). The driveway is new: a single-car-width entrance between the parkway street tree and the tree just inside the lot, curving west to open onto the full carport width. The old driveway, further west in front of the planned porch and lounge, goes away. (No curbs or sidewalks on Laurel Drive, so there's no curb cut to deal with.) The street is a little higher than the carport, so the drive runs downhill toward the house: a trench drain or grading at the carport keeps runoff away from the house.
- Carport: noncombustible framing and finishes; wall space for the EV charger. The exterior meter-main goes where SCE's service point is (the utilities are at the west end), so expect a long run from the panel to the carport charger, or a meter location on the east side if SCE allows.

## Existing conditions (survey)

From the topographic survey in `source_files/survey/` (overlay: [survey-overlay.png](../renders/survey-overlay.png)).

- **Slope:** the lot falls about 8 ft from the east line to the west line, and about 4 ft across the house footprint (high at the carport end, low at the primary-suite end and along the rear). A single flat slab would sit well above grade at the west end. The architect and civil engineer choose the foundation (stepped or raised) and grading approach; drainage must carry water around the house toward the street.
- **Old retaining wall and chimney:** remnants of the previous house. A 2–3 ft retaining wall crosses the house's southeast corner (under the dining area) with a lower terrace behind it, and the old chimney still stands in the planned dining patio. Both come out with demolition, but the grade change they hold remains.
- **East property-line wall:** a retaining wall along the east line from the street back about 43 ft, holding up the neighbour's higher ground; our side is the low side, at its base. Don't excavate or lower the grade along its base on our side, and give the water that seeps through it (weep holes) a path away from the house: a drainage swale or gravel strip along the wall feeding the site drainage. The bin pad and side strip sit beside it.
- **Driveway and trees:** the existing driveway is further west than the planned carport driveway (no curbs on the street, so moving it is simple). The new drive threads between a parkway street tree and a tree just inside the east side of the lot; keep both protected during construction (root zones, no grade change at the trunks).
- **Utilities:** water meter and power pole are at the west end of the frontage (in the parkway); the sewer cleanout is at the front, roughly in line with the porch and lounge.
- **Street:** Laurel Drive right-of-way is 50 ft (25 ft half-width); edge of pavement is about 13 ft in front of the property line.

## Southeast courtyard (behind the carport, east of the house)

- **Dining patio** 12' x 21' along the house wall, from the carport back to the planters, outside the 7' dining slider. 6-seat table oriented north-south.
- **Pavers** 5'-deep band along the back of the carport (keeps the lawn out of the carport's 5' fire zone).
- **Lawn** about 15' x 14' (~210 sq ft), tall fescue + microclover blend, 1' DG edging. Irrigate from the unsoftened line.
- **Raised planters** 3' deep along the rear fence (noncombustible: concrete, block, or steel). Shade-tolerant, dog-safe plants (e.g., coral bells, ferns); no oleander, sago palm, foxglove, or azalea.
- **6' fence** across the back of the carport and bin strip with a **4' gate** behind the carport. The bin strip stays outside the dog yard.
- Optional: one small deciduous tree (e.g., crape myrtle) in the courtyard's southeast corner, ≥10' from the roof; nothing tall south of the house (future solar).

## Trash bins

- **Bin pad 4' x 10'** (four carts) along the east property line in the strip beside the carport, behind the 20' front setback and >5' from the carport.
- Open-top **5' noncombustible screen** on the south and carport sides; open/gated toward the street so carts roll down the strip to the curb.
- Hose bib for rinsing carts on the nearest carport post (unsoftened).

## Dog yard and fences

- Fenced dog yard: the courtyard, the 13' rear yard, and the west side yard.
- **West side yard: 6' fence + 4' gate** across the side yard in line with the front of the house (on the 20' setback line, so full height is allowed). Keep the electric meter on the street side of this fence, or give the meter reader an unlocked, self-closing gate.
- Rear and west side yards: DG, pavers, or groundcover rather than lawn; a DG relief corner at the rear west.

## Heat pump outdoor unit

- Rear yard, against the house's south wall **behind the mechanical closet**, on a raised concrete pad. That stretch of wall has no windows; the nearest are the kitchen sink window (~3½' east) and the laundry window (~4' west), with no bedrooms nearby. The line set runs straight up into the attic beside the mechanical closet.
- Low metal guard around it (dog yard). Keep it ≥10' from the ERV's fresh-air intake. Line set up the wall in a painted metal cover into the attic.
- Pick a quiet model; the unit is only ~9–10' from the rear lot line. Unit plus wall clearance takes ~3' of the 13' rear yard, leaving ~10' to pass.
- Dryer vent at the west end of the laundry wall, away from the unit.

## Front yard (no lawn)

- Native/Mediterranean low-water planting: dry creek bed / rain garden taking the downspouts, boulders, low shrubs screening the bedroom windows.
- **Entry court** in front of the porch, paved, edged on the street side by a seat-height masonry wall (noncombustible) that returns to the house just short of the lounge window. You walk in from the driveway side, and the wall stays low there so you can see when backing out. Lit house numbers on the wall; low-voltage path lights.
- 5' gravel or paver strip along the house; no bark mulch within 30' of the house.
- Driveway: permeable pavers or concrete with gravel strips.
