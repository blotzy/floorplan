## Structural engineer

**Project:** new one-story, wood-frame single-family residence, Eaton Fire rebuild (non-like-for-like: current codes in full), 220 Laurel Dr, Altadena (APN 5832-003-027). About 2,238 sq ft, 72' x 31'-6", Type V-B, sprinklered, Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone. Attached open carport, 18' x 22' (its front 4' a covered walkway).

**Scope requested:** foundation plan, roof and ceiling framing plans, shear wall and hold-down schedule, details, structural calculations (vertical and lateral), truss specifications for the deferred truss submittal, and structural observation. Plans to LA County Building and Safety as the engineer of record.

**What needs engineering (non-prescriptive items):**

- **Roof:** split roof at 2:12, 2'-6" eaves all round: a shed from the porch to the gym/ensuite wall (high at the street) and a gable over the rest of the wing, its ridge down the middle and its rear slope in the shed's plane. The shed's side wall rises above the gable where they meet (the shed's west edge is on the gym/ensuite plumbing wall). Class A asphalt shingles, wind-rated, two layers of underlayment; future solar on rails over flashed attachments. Sealed (unvented) attic with spray foam at the deck.
- **Vaulted great room:** foyer, lounge, dining and kitchen have a sloped ceiling following the roof (rafters, no attic). Elsewhere, flat 9' ceilings with an attic. The vault ends on one straight line (the lounge/gym and kitchen/pantry walls are aligned): the bearing or beam line between the two framing systems.
- **Tall street wall at the lounge:** about 16' at the street, with an 18' clerestory band (3' tall) above a 10' window. Lateral bracing of that wall.
- **Large openings:** dining 12' window (two units) on the south wall, with the 6' dining slider on the east wall near the same corner (about 2'-9" of wall at the corner). Ask whether that corner needs steel or special hold-downs; if so, the window can narrow.
- **Porch:** corner porch notched into the house, roof carried by a corner post; a slim flat metal canopy at 9' over the porch floor's front foot, cantilevering about 1' past the house front.
- **Carport:** attached open carport, clear span with posts at the corners only (about a 22' beam along its open east side), its own roof at about ½" per foot in double-lock standing-seam metal. It faces the north / northeast winds with its open sides, so uplift governs its posts, beam connections, ledger at the house and roof attachment.
- **Loads (located on sheet A-7, attic and loads plan):**
  - Gym rings: two points 20" apart at the centre of the gym, swinging toward the street; 500 lb each, concentrated and dynamic.
  - Attic storage: 20 psf live load on a decked area over the gym, under the shed, from 2' off the street wall to the hall wall.
  - Air handler (about 250 lb) on a platform beside the hall attic hatch, over the guest room, near the gable's ridge.
  - Solar PV over the south-facing planes (the shed and the gable's rear slope, one plane at 2:12 facing SSW, clear of the fire setbacks): about 3-4 psf dead load over the whole plane, so the array can grow. The first array is about 10 kW (about 24 panels) on the shed, east of the range hood cap ([solar layout](../renders/experiments/solar-layout.png)). The **mounting is installed with the roofing** (flashed attachments into the rafters, with rails), the panels later: show the attachment points and spacing, sized for wind uplift in this high-wind area, on the structural sheets.
  - Heat pump water heater, 80 gal (about 900 lb full), on the slab in the mechanical closet.
- **Hall ceiling:** keep the centreline of the bedroom hall (lobby) free of blocking, strongbacks, ducts and pipes, so recessed lights fit between the trusses along it; a 14" tubular skylight near the hall's end takes the last spot, its tube jogging toward the street in the attic to a dome on the street slope (sheet A-7).
- **Exterior:** stucco walls (2x6 with 1" rigid insulation), two 3' metal window canopies at the rear (attachment).

**Wind:** the site sits below the San Gabriel Mountains and gets severe downslope windstorms (Santa Ana and "mono" wind events) from the north and northeast, with gusts of roughly 80 to 100 mph in January 2025. The street side faces about 22° (NNE), straight into them, and it is the tall side of the house: the high edge of the shed roof with its 2'-6" eave, the 16' street wall and the clerestory. Please confirm the design wind speed (LA County's foothill special wind areas), exposure and topographic factor, and check uplift at the street eave and the overhangs, the open carport roof (standing seam: clip spacing at edges and corners), the porch, the shingle roof's edges and the future solar attachments. Windows and the clerestory need design-pressure ratings to suit.

**Foundation:** the existing grade falls about 4' across the footprint (east to west). Slab on grade preferred; stepped footings or a raised portion as the civil and structural engineers decide together. An old retaining wall and chimney under the southeast corner are being removed. Ask whether a soils report is needed.

## Civil engineer

**Project:** as above. Lot 113.00' x 64.77' (7,319 sq ft), topographic survey on hand (DWG, DXF, PDF; sent separately).

**Scope requested:** finished floor elevation, grading and drainage plan, erosion control for construction, and the County grading permit determination (Appendix J103). Coordinate the foundation-to-grade design with the structural engineer.

**Site conditions and design inputs:**

- **Slope:** about 8' of fall across the lot from east to west and about 4' across the house footprint. The house sits near the minimum setbacks on three sides (front 1¼", rear 2½", west about 2⅜" of margin), so the foundation location needs a survey check before the slab.
- **Demolition:** remove the old retaining wall crossing the house's southeast corner and the standing chimney in the courtyard; regrade the lower rear terrace they leave.
- **East property-line wall:** a retaining wall along the east line (front half), holding up the neighbour's higher ground. No excavation or lowering of grade at its base on our side; a swale or gravel drain along it carries its seepage to the site drainage.
- **Drainage:** grade away from the house all round (CRC R401.3). Three downspouts along the rear gutter, plus the carport gutter on its east edge. The bedroom wing's street eave has no gutter: a gravel drip trench along the wall under it, with a drain to the site system. An ember-resistant zone (gravel, pavers or low irrigated ground cover) under the eaves and 5' beyond them.
- **Driveway:** new, straight at the carport's full 18' width from the street to the carport (the two east parkway trees, an olive and a ficus, and a dead lot tree in its way are removed); drained away from the carport. Public Works approach permit.
- **Utilities:** sewer from the west-end baths to the existing cleanout near the porch, at code slope (lot falls to the west: confirm it works). **Public sewer and street grade** (LA County Public Works, Oct 2026; [1952 as-built, County Improvement 1324, sheet 10-C](../renders/county/laurel-sewer-as-built-1952.png)): an 8" clay main in Laurel Drive between manholes at station 6+00 (invert 1417.07) and 9+33.70 (invert 1429.14), slope 3.68%; the lot (original lot 11, 113' frontage) is at roughly stations 7+57 to 8+70, so the main's invert along the frontage is about 1423 to 1427. The street (curb grade) rises about 3.8% toward Glenrose Ave, about 4' across the frontage: useful for the driveway approach grades. The 1952 plan shows no Laurel lateral to this lot (it was served from Las Flores Drive); where the existing lateral goes is being located. All 1952 elevations need verifying in the field and tying to the survey datum. Water: existing 5/8" meter (Lincoln Avenue Water; 1" service on their side); a 1" meter and a backflow preventer are likely for the sprinklers. Electric service at the northwest corner (SCE). No gas.
- **Trees:** protect the parkway olives' root zones.
