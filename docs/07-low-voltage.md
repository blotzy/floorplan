# Low-voltage and network

[← Back to README](../README.md)

All structured cabling home-runs to the network rack (in the mechanical / network closet), in smurf tube (flexible conduit) so cables can be replaced or added later.

- **Rooms:** two smurf-tube runs with two Cat6A cables each in every room, except the dining area and kitchen.
- **Office:** four Cat6A drops at the desk wall plus two on a second wall.
- **Wi-Fi access points:** two ceiling-mounted PoE access points, one in the bedroom hall (between the primary and the office) and one where the hall opens into the great room. Run two Cat6A cables to each.
- **Internet service entry:** conduit from the street or utility service point to the network rack, for fiber or cable.
- **Cameras:** PoE exterior cameras. Include drops at the carport, the corner porch, the bin pad, and under the rear eave.
- **Doorbell:** PoE doorbell at the corner porch. Confirm the chosen model's chime wiring (some PoE models use a digital chime and need no transformer). Feed can show on the lounge TV.
- **TV wall:** a 2" conduit through the foyer wall from the recessed TV box to the **AV closet** (the foyer closet at the dining-room end, directly behind the TV), for HDMI, speaker and control cables; a conduit from the AV closet to the network rack.
- **Front speakers (in-wall):** left and right in the TV wall flanking the TV, and a center above it, each in a back-box; in-wall rated speaker wire (CL2/CL3, 14- or 16-gauge) from each to the AV closet, labelled at both ends. Set the left and right at about ear height or angled in-ceiling-style models toward the seating; the center just above the TV.
- **AV closet:** a data drop and a shelf for the receiver, streamers and consoles (wireless controllers; an IR repeater to the TV box if any remote needs line of sight).
- **Rear speakers (in-wall):** two in the lounge side of the gym wall, behind the couch at about ear height, one to each side of the seating, each in a back-box (the gym is on the other side; keep them clear of the gym's equipment blocking); speaker wire from each to the AV closet, labelled at both ends.
- **Lounge subwoofers:** a subwoofer cable (or a small conduit with a pull string) from the AV closet to a low wall plate in each of the lounge's two street-wall corners: by the TV wall (beside the hearth bench's window end) and by the gym wall (at the couch's end). Start with one sub; the second evens out the bass across the seating.
- **Primary bedroom TV:** a low-voltage pass-through at the recessed TV box on the wall facing the bed, with a data drop (or a conduit to the attic).
- **Attic access:** a conduit from the network rack into the attic for future runs.
- **Optional:** low-voltage or power at window heads for motorized shades.
