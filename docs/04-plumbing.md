# Plumbing

[← Back to README](../README.md)

The water service enters at the northwest corner (the utilities are at the west end). Unsoftened lines branch off first, then a single main runs to the mechanical closet, where everything downstream is softened.

Interior supply piping is copper. No PEX except for the fire sprinklers (a standalone NFPA 13D system; sprinklers are required for all new single-family homes, including fire rebuilds).

**Water service order:** meter → main shutoff and pressure regulator → smart leak-detection shutoff valve → unsoftened branches → main line to the mechanical closet → sediment pre-filter → softener → water heater and manifold.

| Line | Water | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire sprinklers | Unsoftened, direct from main | Sprinkler designer to confirm service/meter size (often 1"); plans due by foundation sign-off |
| Hose bibs and irrigation | Unsoftened | Tee before the softener; includes the courtyard lawn and the bin-pad hose bib |

**Bathrooms**

- Use epoxy or pre-mixed urethane grout for shower tile work.
- Powder room toilet sits on the wall shared with the laundry alcove (washer/sink on the other side).

**Kitchen**

- Integrated drain board on the dishwasher side of the sink (countertop with a slight slope and grooves).
- Counter-depth fridge to avoid pinching the walkway.
- Drink fridge in place of the fourth peninsula stool (the plan now has three stools).
- Coffee station on the counter over the drink fridge.

**Equipment and fixtures**

- Whole-house water softener with sediment pre-filter, in the mechanical closet. The softener needs a drain with an air gap.
- Heat pump water heater in the mechanical closet, with a condensate drain and a drain pan with a leak sensor. Size it for the house; go to **80 gal** if the two-person tub happens (see below).
- Tankless reverse-osmosis filter under the kitchen sink, feeding the sink tap and a line to the fridge. A bypass plug replaces the fridge's internal filter.
- Leak pans under the washer and fridge.
- Bidet seats at the three full baths. Plan toilet locations so each bidet outlet has enough separation from tubs and showers.
- Laundry sink beside the washer in the laundry alcove.

**Optional**

- Reading nook with built-in bench in the primary bedroom.
- **72" two-person tub in the common bath.** Fits the 6'-0" width as an alcove tub if the alcove is framed to the tub's spec (typically 72"–72½" stud to stud); pick the model before framing. Fill is 80–110+ gal and the filler is limited to 120°F (CPC 409.4), so it needs an 80-gal heat pump water heater or a 140°F tank with a mixing valve. A slab floor handles the ~1,400 lb filled load. Jetted/air tubs add a dedicated GFCI circuit and an access panel.
- On-demand hot water recirculation to the primary bath, the run farthest from the water heater.
