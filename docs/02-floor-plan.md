# Architect: floor plan, roof, finishes

[← Back to README](../README.md)

These are the rooms, spaces, and openings the plan must provide so the trades can do their work. Dimensions are from the current owner-edits DXF; see the [plan change log](10-plan-changes.md) for how each one got there.

## Entry, foyer, and lounge

- **Corner porch** at the lounge/carport (northeast) corner: 7'-4" x 4'-0" notch with a 6x6 post at the outside corner where the carport beam lands. Front step in front.
- **Front door** on the street-facing side of the notch: 36" door plus a 14" sidelight.
- **Reeded-glass window** (about 24") in the porch's side wall, so you can see who's at the door from the lounge. Narrow reeds or satin-reeded, dual-pane, low-E, tempered, NFRC-rated, from the same window line as the rest of the house.
- **Foyer** 6'-8" x 7'-4" with a closet and bench on the east wall. The **foyer wall** (about 12' long, stopping short of the lobby) separates foyer and lounge; its lounge side is the **TV wall**.
- **Lounge** 21'-2" x 17'-2": 10' north window centred on the wall; loveseat floated 18" off the window, sofa facing the TV wall, 30" round coffee table, two small chairs on the south side, all kept north of the walkway from the foyer to the bedroom hall.

## Dining

- **Dining** 14'-6" x 13'-2"; table and the south window centred on the dining room's south wall.
- **7' sliding door** on the east wall, centred between the carport fence and the house's southeast corner, opening onto the dining patio. (The designer's console in front of the old east window is gone.)
- **South window:** large (drawn 10'). Final size is the architect's call; it faces south, so plan an overhang or exterior shade and check it against the Title 24 glazing budget.

## Kitchen

See [kitchen](08-kitchen.md). The plan reserves a 5'-0" work aisle and a peninsula with three stools facing the dining area.

## Pantry, laundry, mechanical, powder (the block behind the kitchen)

Current main plan (the side hall is kept; see the experiments below for alternatives):

- **Pantry** 7'-9" x 5' (2' wider than the designer's). Door off the main lobby at the corner by the fridge, hinged so it opens flat against the kitchen wall. Outlets inside.
- **Laundry** 7'-9" x 7'-2" (2' wider), door off the side hall. Dryer, washer (side by side, not stacked) and laundry sink on the south wall; 20"-deep folding counter on the pantry wall with 36" clear in front of the appliances. Leak pan under the washer.
- **Mechanical / network closet** (the designer's hallway closet), about 3'-6" x 8'-6", off the side hall, behind louvered bifolds. Holds the heat pump water heater, softener and brine tank, sediment pre-filter, water distribution manifold, and the network rack. Keep the rack at the lobby end, partitioned from the water equipment, with no water lines in its walls or ceiling. Floor drain or drain pan.
- **Powder room** 6'-11" x 3'-8" at the end of the side hall. Toilet turned to sit on the primary-bath wet wall, facing down the room's length (24"+ clear in front per CPC 402.5).
- **Side hall** (3'-5") stays a clear 36" walkway.
- **Indoor electrical subpanel** flush in the laundry-side wall of the side hall, across from the mechanical closet. The hall provides the 30" x 36" working space; keep the washer's plumbing out of that section of wall.
- **Robot vacuum dock** niche at the end of the side hall or in the laundry's toe-kick, near the washer drain, open to the floor.

## Bedroom wing

- **Bedroom 2 (10'-6" x 12'-6"): gym.** North window added (it had none); matches bedroom 4's. LVP with loose-laid rubber mats. Mineral wool in the walls shared with the lounge/nook end. ERV boost control. Outlet for a stationary bike.
- **Gym ring mounts:** two ceiling points about 20" apart near the room's centre, swinging along the 12'-6" direction. The truss designer must carry a concentrated load of about 500 lb at each point; doubled 2x or LVL blocking across 2–3 trusses with framing connectors; through-bolted forged eye bolts with plate washers. Keep lights, fans, HVAC/ERV registers, and the smoke alarm out of the 4–6' swing zone. Photograph the blocking before drywall.
- **Bedroom 3 (12'-8" x 12'-6"): guest room.** Queen bed, blackout shades. The designer drew two 4' closets flanking a centred door; see [open questions](09-open-questions.md).
- **Bedroom 4 (10'-6" x 12'-6"): office.** Solid-core door and insulated walls for calls; wall blocking for monitor arms and shelves; dimmable ceiling light.
- **Common bath (6'-0" x 12'-6").** Optional 72" two-person alcove tub; the 6' width works if the alcove is framed to the tub's spec (pick the model before framing). See [plumbing](04-plumbing.md).
- **Primary suite:** optional reading nook with a built-in bench (window seat) in the primary bedroom.

## Ceilings and roof

- 10' ceilings throughout. The ducted heat pump unit, its ducts, and the ERV go in the sealed attic, so no hallway drop ceiling is needed.
- Ceiling box or low-voltage bracket at the two Wi-Fi access point locations, installed at rough-in with the Cat6A ending inside.
- Ceiling fans need 12–18" downrods at 10'.
- Keep the south-facing roof free of vents and skylights for future solar; no tall trees south of the house.
- Solar mounts and conduit on the roof, plus a conduit run to a future battery location.
- **Class A asphalt shingle roof** over the sealed attic (unvented deck allowed by CRC R806.5). Get the roofer to confirm in writing that the shingle warranty covers an unvented deck; a light-coloured cool-roof shingle is preferred.

## Sealed attic and storage

- Insulation at the roofline: spray foam under the roof deck or rigid board above it. No attic vents.
- Attic storage loading (about 20 psf) on a central strip, specified to the truss designer, with plywood decking over it.
- Insulated pull-down ladder sized for 10' ceilings (about a 30" x 54" opening), in the hallway or laundry. Light switch at the hatch and an outlet in the attic.
- Equipment access per mechanical code: at least a 22" x 30" opening, a 24"-wide walkway, and a 30" x 30" service platform with a light and outlet at the ducted unit and ERV. Place that equipment near the hatch.
- Thermal barrier over the spray foam (½" drywall or an approved coating), since the attic will be used for storage.
- Smurf tube or a cable tray across the attic from the network rack for future runs.
- Keep heavy items and heat-producing equipment such as servers out of the attic.

## Wildfire hardening

- Ember-resistant vents and enclosed eaves.
- Noncombustible siding and tempered dual-pane windows.
- A 5' noncombustible zone around the house (pavers/gravel, no mulch or turf).
- Sealed (unvented) attic with no attic vents, so embers have no entry point.
- Metal (or other noncombustible) fencing for at least the first 5' where any fence meets the house.
