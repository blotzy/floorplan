## Oct 18, 2026: changes from Oct 17

**Roof**
- The roof is now a split roof at 2:12 instead of a single shed over the whole house. A shed runs from the porch to the gym/ensuite wall: it is high at the street, covers the whole great room and the gym, and keeps the 18' x 3' clerestory over the lounge. A low gable covers the rest of the bedroom wing, with its ridge down the middle of the house and its rear slope in the shed's plane.
- Roofing is now dark Class A asphalt shingles, wind-rated, on two layers of underlayment, instead of standing-seam metal. Future solar mounts on rails over flashed attachments. The carport keeps its double-lock standing-seam roof, since it's too flat for shingles.
- Gutters: one along the rear. The bedroom wing's street eave has no gutter; it drips to a gravel trench with a drain.
- Roof penetrations:
  - The guest ensuite's plumbing vent and the hall skylight's dome are on the gable's street slope, off the solar side.
  - The other fixtures share one vent just downslope of the common bath skylight.
  - The range hood keeps its roof cap.
- Attic storage (20 psf, decked) is now over the gym, under the shed. Before, it was a strip over the bedroom wing. The air handler platform sits beside the hatch, near the gable's ridge.
- The street-wall trim band is gone. The street facade's articulation is now the recessed windows, and there's a planner question about whether that is enough.
- 3D Roof menu: the plan's split roof, plus three alternatives: a gable with flat ceilings, a split with the shed over the lounge only, and the previous shed over the whole house. All are shown in shingles.

**Bedroom hall and primary suite**
- The west exterior door and its glass transom are gone. The hall ends at a plain wall just past the office and primary doors.
- A cased opening with a dropped header (7'-6") crosses the hall in line with the guest ensuite wall, marking the bedroom end. The guest door moved 2" west to make room for its trim.
- A 14" tubular skylight is centred in the hall near its end. In the attic its tube jogs toward the street to a dome on the gable's street slope.
- The hall's last stretch is now the primary bedroom's reading nook. It has a built-in bench along the office wall and a 3'-0" x 4'-0" window in the west wall.
- The attic access is a 22" x 54" insulated access panel instead of a pull-down ladder. It moved about 2' east, clear of the skylight, and the hall smoke/CO alarm moved with it.

**Other rooms**
- Guest ensuite: reached only from the guest room. Its hall door is gone, and a wall-to-wall linen closet opening into the bath takes its place.
- Laundry: no exhaust fan or wall cap. The dryer is ventless, the heat pump water heater dries the air, and the high window opens.
- Ceiling airer removed from the plans (a post-construction finish).
- Dining slider back to 8'.

**Site**
- Carport: 18' x 20', its front in line with the house front and the porch, so the walk to the porch stays under cover. Lot coverage is about 2,628 sq ft (35.9%).
- Courtyard gate behind the carport: a 14' metal gate, hinged at the house corner, that swings into the carport and opens flat against the house wall, so the carport and courtyard become one space for parties. It was a 4' gate.
- The EV charger moved to the house wall at the carport's street end, clear of the open gate.

**Energy**
- Glazing is about 453 sq ft, about 20.2% of the floor area. That is just over the 20% prescriptive limit, so the energy consultant needs the performance path, or a little glass comes out.
